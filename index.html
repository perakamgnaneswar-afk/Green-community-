About Us

Green Community is a Community Engagement Programme that helps neighbours take simple, shared steps to protect the environment where they live.

🎯 Our Mission

To inspire people to take collective action towards a cleaner, greener, and more sustainable neighbourhood.

🌍 Our Vision

To create an environmentally responsible community where every individual contributes to protecting nature.

Our Key Objectives

📚

Environmental Awareness

Help people understand how daily habits affect nature.

🤝

Community Participation

Bring neighbours together for drives, workshops and events.

♻️

Sustainable Living

Encourage lasting choices that reduce waste and save resources.

Our Activities

Choose a way to get involved. Select “Learn More” on any card for details.

🌳

Tree Plantation

Encourage people to plant and care for trees in their neighbourhood.

Pick native species, plant in groups, and water new saplings regularly for their first year. Volunteers can adopt a tree and track its growth.
🧹

Cleanliness Drives

Organise community clean-up activities in parks, streets, and public spaces.

Teams bring gloves and bags, clean one zone, and sort the waste they collect. Drives usually last two to three hours.
🗑️

Waste Management

Educate people about waste segregation and responsible disposal.

Keep wet (kitchen) waste and dry (paper, plastic, metal) waste in separate bins. Compost wet waste where possible.
♻️

Recycling Campaigns

Promote recycling of paper, plastic, glass, and other reusable materials.

Collection points for paper, glass and clean plastics, with guides on what each local recycler accepts.
🛍️

Plastic-Free Campaign

Encourage reusable bags, bottles, and eco-friendly alternatives.

Swap single-use items for cloth bags, steel bottles and refillable containers. Local shops can join as plastic-free partners.
📣

Environmental Awareness

Conduct workshops, rallies, and educational programmes about sustainability.

School talks, street plays and hands-on workshops that explain climate, water, waste and biodiversity in simple terms.
